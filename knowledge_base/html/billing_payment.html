Billing & Payment

AutoPay setup, late fees, fee waiver eligibility, updating your card, and invoice management.

Billing Cycle

Orbit bills monthly on the anniversary of your account creation date. If you signed up on the 15th, your invoice generates on the 15th of every subsequent month. There is no annual billing option — all plans are month-to-month.

AutoPay

AutoPay is the recommended setting for all accounts. When enabled, your card on file is charged automatically on your billing date. You receive an email receipt within 24 hours of a successful charge.

To enable AutoPay: Settings → Billing → Payment Method → toggle AutoPay ON.

You may disable AutoPay at any time. If AutoPay is off, you are responsible for paying your invoice manually before the due date to avoid suspension.

Important: If your card on file has expired, AutoPay will fail. Orbit notifies you by email, but an unpaid balance may result in account suspension within 3 days. Keep your card details current to avoid interruption.

Manual Payments

If you prefer not to use AutoPay, you can pay your monthly invoice manually. When AutoPay is off, Orbit emails you an invoice on your billing date. You have 3 days to submit payment before a late fee is applied and your account is flagged for suspension review.

To make a manual payment: Settings → Billing → Pay Now, or contact our virtual assistant — provide your account ID and we can process the payment instantly against your card on file or a new card.

Note: Accounts with AutoPay off are not eligible for the late fee waiver (see below). The waiver requires AutoPay to be enabled at the time of the missed payment. If fee waiver eligibility matters to you, we recommend enabling AutoPay in Settings → Billing.

Late Fees

If payment is not received within 3 days of your billing date, a late fee is added to your pending balance. The amount depends on your plan:

Late fees are non-negotiable except where an Orbit system error caused the failure, or when the account qualifies for a one-time fee waiver (see below).

Fee Waiver Eligibility

Orbit may waive the late fee once per 12-month period. All three conditions below must be met:

  • Account tenure: Your account has been active for more than 6 months
  • AutoPay was enabled: AutoPay was turned on at the time of the missed payment
  • No prior waiver in the last 12 months: A waiver has not been applied to this account within the past 12 months

All three must be satisfied. If any one condition is not met, the late fee applies in full. Partial waivers are not available. Fee waiver eligibility is assessed automatically when you request account reactivation — you do not need to apply separately.

Tip: The best way to ensure fee waiver eligibility is to keep AutoPay enabled. If your card expires, update it promptly to avoid both the suspension and the loss of AutoPay status.

Updating Your Payment Method

You can update your credit or debit card at any time from Settings → Billing → Payment Method. Changes take effect immediately and will be used for any outstanding balance or the next scheduled charge.

If you are restoring a suspended account and your card on file is expired, you will be asked to provide a new card before payment is processed. The new card becomes your default card on file for future charges.

Pending Balance

If a payment fails, the unpaid amount becomes your pending balance. Orbit retries the charge automatically once after 24 hours. If the retry also fails, the account is flagged for suspension review.

Your pending balance must be settled in full before service can be restored following a suspension. Partial payments are not accepted.

Invoices and Receipts

All payment receipts are emailed to the account owner's email address on file within 24 hours of a successful charge. Past invoices can be downloaded from Settings → Billing → Invoice History. Invoices are retained for 24 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

My AutoPay failed because my card expired. Will I be charged a late fee?

If the card expiry caused the failure, a late fee will appear on your balance. However, if your account has been with us for more than 6 months, had AutoPay enabled, and has not used a waiver in the past 12 months, the fee will be waived automatically when you restore your account.

I missed a payment but AutoPay was turned off. Can I get a fee waiver?

No. One of the three waiver conditions requires that AutoPay was enabled at the time of the missed payment. If AutoPay was off, the late fee applies regardless of account age or waiver history.

How do I know if I qualify for a fee waiver before I pay?

Our virtual assistant checks your eligibility automatically when you initiate a restore. You'll be informed of the waiver outcome before you confirm payment, so you know the exact total you'll be charged.

Can I pay a partial balance to restore my account?

No. The full pending balance must be settled in a single payment before your account can be restored. Partial or installment payments are not supported.

I pay manually every month. Can I still use Orbit without AutoPay?

Yes — AutoPay is recommended but not required. If AutoPay is off, you are responsible for submitting payment within 3 days of your invoice date to avoid late fees and suspension. Our virtual assistant can process your payment instantly — just provide your account ID and confirm payment.

I pay manually and have a great payment history. Why can't I get a fee waiver?

The fee waiver is specifically designed for accounts where AutoPay was enabled but failed due to an expired card — essentially a system-side failure on an otherwise reliable account. When AutoPay is deliberately turned off, the responsibility to pay on time rests with the account holder, so the waiver condition cannot be met. Enabling AutoPay (Settings → Billing → Payment Method) protects your eligibility going forward.
