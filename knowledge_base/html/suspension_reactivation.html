Suspension & Reactivation

Why accounts are suspended, what remains accessible during suspension, and how to restore your service.

Why Accounts Get Suspended

An Orbit account is suspended when a payment fails and the outstanding balance is not settled within 3 days. This can happen when:

  • A credit or debit card on file has expired
  • AutoPay is disabled and a manual payment was not made by the due date
  • A card is declined due to insufficient funds or bank restrictions
  • The card was updated with incorrect details

Orbit sends email reminders before suspending an account. If a payment fails, you receive an immediate notification, followed by a second notice 24 hours before suspension takes effect.

What Happens During Suspension

While your account is suspended:

  • Team members cannot log in or access any projects
  • Automations and integrations are paused
  • Your data is retained for up to 30 days from the suspension date (see the Data & Storage article for details)
  • You cannot invite new members or make plan changes
  • The account owner can still log in to settle the balance and initiate restoration

Data risk after 30 days: If a suspended account remains unpaid for more than 30 days, projects may be archived or permanently deleted. Restore your account within 30 days to ensure all data is preserved.

How to Restore Your Account

The fastest way to restore your account is through our virtual assistant. Here is what happens step by step:

Confirm your account

Provide your 5-digit account ID. The assistant retrieves your account status, balance, and data retention window.

Data safety check

The assistant confirms whether your projects are still within the 30-day retention window and reports how many projects are on file.

Review your balance and late fee

Your pending balance and any applicable late fee are disclosed upfront. If eligible, the late fee is waived automatically.

Provide or confirm payment

If your card on file is valid, you can confirm payment directly. If it is expired, you will be asked to provide a new card number. The new card replaces the expired one for all future charges.

Account restored

Once payment is processed, your account is restored immediately. All team members can log in and access projects right away. A confirmation receipt is sent to the email on file.

One-step restore: If your account was suspended due to an expired card, simply provide your new card number and give consent to pay. The assistant handles the rest — card update, payment, late fee check, and restore — in a single transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I restore my account without paying the full balance?

No. The full pending balance must be paid in a single transaction before the account can be restored. Partial payments are not accepted.

My account has been suspended for over 30 days. What should I do?

After 30 days, some or all project data may have been archived or deleted. Contact our support team before initiating a restore — a data recovery specialist can advise you on what is recoverable. Restoring without consulting the team will not recover already-deleted data.

How long does restoration take after I pay?

Restoration is immediate. Once your payment is confirmed, your account status updates within seconds. Team members can log in right away.

Will I be charged for the days my account was suspended?

No. Orbit does not prorate billing during suspension periods. Your billing cycle resets from the date of restoration.

Can I cancel instead of restoring?

Yes. Contact our support team and they will walk you through the cancellation process. Note that canceling a suspended account does not require settling the balance in most cases — our team will advise on your specific situation.
