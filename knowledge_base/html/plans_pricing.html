Plans & Pricing

Everything you need to know about Orbit's four plan tiers, what's included, and how to choose the right one for your team.

Overview

Orbit offers four plans for teams of every size. All plans are billed monthly, per account — not per user seat. Your charge is the same fixed amount regardless of how many seats you fill, up to your plan's maximum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is pricing per user or per account?

Pricing is per account, not per user. Your monthly charge is fixed at the plan rate regardless of how many seats you fill, up to your plan's maximum. For example, a Team plan account with 3 users still pays $49/month.

Can I add more seats beyond my plan limit?

No. Each plan has a hard seat maximum. To accommodate more users, you must upgrade to the next plan tier. Individual seats cannot be purchased as add-ons.

Is there a free trial?

Orbit does not currently offer a free trial. All plans are available on a month-to-month basis with no long-term commitment or cancellation fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

Orbit accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We do not currently accept PayPal, wire transfers, or invoicing for monthly plans.

Do prices include taxes?

Displayed prices exclude applicable taxes. Your final invoice will include any taxes required by your jurisdiction.

Can I upgrade to Business for just a few months and then revert?

Yes. When requesting a plan upgrade through your account or our virtual assistant, you can specify a duration (for example, "upgrade to Business for 3 months"). The plan reverts automatically at the end of that period. See our Upgrades & Downgrades article for details.
