Cancellation & Offboarding

How to cancel your Orbit account, export your data before you leave, and what to expect after cancellation.

Before You Cancel

If you're thinking about canceling, our team would love the chance to help. Many customers who reach out to cancel are dealing with an issue we can resolve — a billing problem, a missing feature, or a question about their plan. You can reach us via the virtual assistant or at support@orbit.io.

If you're certain you'd like to cancel, here's what to do before your account is closed.

Export your data first. After cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days and then permanently deleted. Export everything you need before submitting a cancellation request.

How to Export Your Data

From your active account, go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Export. Your export includes:

  • All projects, tasks, subtasks, and comments
  • File attachments and uploaded media
  • Member and permission settings
  • Integration configuration (export only — connections are deactivated at cancellation)

Exports are delivered as a compressed ZIP archive to the account owner's email address within 2 hours of request. For large accounts (over 50 GB), allow up to 24 hours.

How to Cancel

  1. Export your data (see above)
  2. Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Account
  3. Confirm your cancellation request
  4. You will receive a cancellation confirmation email within a few minutes

Your account remains accessible until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, access is revoked and the cancellation is final.

Suspended accounts: If your account is currently suspended, you cannot cancel through Settings. Contact our support team directly and they will process the cancellation for you. In most cases, you do not need to settle an outstanding balance to cancel a suspended account — our team will advise on your specific situation.

What Happens After Cancellation

Once your account is canceled:

  • All team members immediately lose access
  • Integrations and automations are deactivated
  • Your data is retained for 30 days and then permanently deleted
  • No further charges are made
  • Your account ID is reserved in our system but cannot be reactivated — a new account would need to be created

Refund Policy

Orbit does not offer refunds for unused portions of a billing cycle. If you cancel mid-cycle, your account remains accessible until the cycle ends, but no partial refund is issued. We do not offer refunds on late fees or setup charges.

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact our billing team at billing@orbit.io and we will investigate within 5 business days.

Thinking about coming back?

Former Orbit customers are always welcome. Since canceled accounts cannot be reactivated, you would start fresh with a new account. Our sales team can help you select the right plan and, in some cases, arrange a courtesy onboarding for returning customers. Reach out at sales@orbit.io or speak to our virtual assistant and ask about rejoining.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reactivate my account after canceling?

No. Canceled accounts cannot be reactivated. If you want to return to Orbit, you would create a new account. Note that a new account starts fresh — prior project data from the canceled account will not be available unless you exported it before cancellation.

Will I be charged for the month after I cancel?

No. Once you cancel, no further charges are made. You retain access through the end of the current billing cycle, and then the account is closed with no additional charge.

How long is my data kept after cancellation?

Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation and then permanently deleted. This is the same 30-day window that applies to suspended accounts. Download your data export before or immediately after canceling to ensure nothing is lost.

Can I pause my account instead of canceling?

Orbit does not currently offer an account pause feature. If cost is the concern, downgrading to the Individual plan at $10/month may be a lower-cost option while you decide. Contact our team and we can discuss your options.
